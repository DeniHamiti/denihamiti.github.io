About

What data analytics means to me

To me, data analytics is less about numbers on a screen and more about listening carefully—to patterns, outliers, and the stories hiding in plain sight. It’s a way of pursuing knowledge with rigor, then translating what I find so others can use it.

As a data analyst at the Massachusetts Department of Public Health, I apply that mindset to high-stakes public health work: exploring messy datasets in SQL, R, and Python, and building Tableau dashboards that help partners see what matters and act on it.

My background in chemistry and mathematics from UMass Amherst shaped how I approach every project—with curiosity, precision, and a belief that good analysis should always be understandable.
